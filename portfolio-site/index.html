Case 02 — AI travel assistant (NDA)
Key decision

Conversation as the interface

Travellers say what they need. The assistant turns a spoken request into a trip summary, asks only for what's missing and returns results with the details that matter.

  • Speech-to-text trip prompts
  • Trip details as editable pills
  • Answers with next actions, like AR navigation
Case 02 — AI travel assistant (NDA)

When the assistant answers "where", the camera shows the way. AR guides travellers through the airport and the cabin, with live updates along the route.

  • Carry-on check with live measurements
  • Turn-by-turn directions with nearby stops
  • Delay alerts and gate timeline in context
  • Guidance down the aisle to the seat

Case 03 — Factory SCADA for Qt Group (NDA)
Context & role

Make an unfamiliar industrial process intuitive for every role.

Context

Operators, engineers and managers monitor production lines, energy and supply-chain KPIs on a control-room wall, a touch controller and mobile.

Constraints

Little domain knowledge to start with, deciding which data needs real-time monitoring, and different workflows per role.

My role

Planned and designed the end-to-end UI with client stakeholders, and prototyped key flows to validate the concept.

Case 03 — Factory SCADA for Qt Group (NDA)
Key decision

Role-based dashboards built from modular widgets

Each role gets a dashboard assembled from a shared widget library: current status, a live notification feed, real-time charts and KPIs. New metrics fit in without a redesign, and dark and light themes adapt it to different environments.

Case 03 — Factory SCADA for Qt Group (NDA)
Key decision

A touch controller for the whole factory

Operators drive the control-room wall from a touch device: pick a line on the 2D factory plan, choose which data to highlight, and rotate or zoom the 3D view with a gizmo.

  • 2D plan of every line and device
  • Widget controls and date range
  • 3D gizmo for the wall view
Case 03 — Factory SCADA for Qt Group (NDA)
Design system

A dedicated design system for industrial UI

To keep a multi-screen product consistent, I built its own UI kit: Hubot Sans typography, dark and light colour tokens, status colours and a library of data widgets reused across every device.

Why it matters

New metrics and screens are assembled from existing components, the basis of a modular architecture that kept delivery on schedule.

Case 03 — Factory SCADA for Qt Group (NDA)
Multi-device

Control room, touch controller and mobile

The system scales from a three-screen control-room wall to a touch controller for the 3D and 2D factory view, and to mobile for monitoring and alerts on the go.

Outcome

Positive stakeholder feedback on core requirements. Delivered on schedule by prioritising critical features. Modular architecture ready for future metrics.
